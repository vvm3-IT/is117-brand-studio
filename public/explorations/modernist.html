Reference & choices

Josef Müller-Brockmann, Musica Viva, 1958

A large pale geometric field and small repeated squares make rhythm visible in the reference. Here, a precise two-column grid, repeated square marks, and one sans-serif type family turn that rhythm into steady training effort. Flat color and simple rules echo economical print production.

Intended effect

The steady hierarchy may support your predicted chill feeling and make the next action easy to find.

Risk to check

The restraint could feel too impersonal for a beginner looking for encouragement.
