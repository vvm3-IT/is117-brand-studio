Quiet Reps — a study in showing up.

Behind the direction

Quiet has layers.

Same shirt. Same headline, support, and action. Different visual thinking.

Reference & choices

Wolfgang Weingart, Das Schweizer Plakat (The Swiss Poster) 1900–1984, 1983

The reference layers halftone textures, torn-looking shapes, and typography. This interpretation mixes a serif headline with practical sans-serif labels, offsets paper-like fields, and layers a dotted texture behind the shirt. It quotes printed training notes while keeping the interface on a stable reading plane.

Intended effect

The contrast between a quiet message and expressive layers may make dedication feel personal and welcoming.

Risk to check

The extra layers may compete with the chill feeling you want; on narrow screens they are reduced to preserve reading order.
