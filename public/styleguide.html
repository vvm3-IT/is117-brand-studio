00 / Direction

Decisions first.

Quiet dedication for bodybuilders, with encouragement for beginners. These audiences are hypotheses, not validated research.

Brand brief / working hypotheses

Audience

Quiet dedication

Bodybuilders heading to the gym; beginners looking for motivation.

Archetype

Hero

Effort, courage, and improvement expressed without bravado.

Visual direction

Layered calm

Postmodernist direction: paper-like layers and mixed type, with soft sage and lavender.

06 / Voice

Words are
design, too.

Calm, determined, encouraging. Invite effort without shaming someone or making performance promises.

Brand voice example

“Show up. Put in the reps.”

Short, direct, and specific about the next action.

The promise

A quiet reminder to show up and put in the work. Avoid invented results, scarcity, testimonials, or unsupported product claims.

07 / Persuasion

Useful. Voluntary.

Reciprocity

A free pre-session intention ritual provides something useful without purchase or sign-up. Its benefit is proposed, not a proven conversion effect.

Commitment and consistency

The optional intention selector lets the visitor make a small personal commitment. The response invites them to carry it forward, with the shirt available as an optional reminder. No selection adds an item or pressures a purchase; required size selection is not this mechanism.
